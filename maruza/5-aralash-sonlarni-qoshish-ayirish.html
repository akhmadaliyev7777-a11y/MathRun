Aralash sonlarni qo'shish va ayirish

Maxrajlari har xil kasrlarni qo'shish va ayirishni eslaymiz, so'ng aralash sonlarni qo'shish, ayirish va javobni chamalab tekshirishni o'rganamiz.

1Eslab olaylik — kasrlarni qo'shish va ayirish

Choraklar bilan uchdan birlarni qanday qo'shaman? Bo'laklari har xil-ku!
Eslab olaylik
  • Maxrajlari teng kasrlarni qo'shganda (ayirganda) suratlar qo'shiladi (ayiriladi), maxraj o'zgarmaydi: \(\frac{2}{7} + \frac{3}{7} = \frac{5}{7}\).
  • Maxrajlari har xil bo'lsa, avval kasrlar umumiy maxrajga keltiriladi. Umumiy maxraj — ikkala maxrajga ham bo'linadigan eng kichik son (maxrajlarning eng kichik umumiy karralisi).
  • Kasrni yangi maxrajga keltirish uchun surat ham, maxraj ham bir xil songa ko'paytiriladi.
  • Javob noto'g'ri kasr bo'lsa, aralash son qilib yoziladi; qisqarsa, qisqartiriladi.
Namuna · \(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4}\)
13 va 4 ning eng kichik umumiy karralisi — 12.
2\(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \nat{\frac{11}{12}}\)

Ayirish: \(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{10}{12} - \frac{3}{12} = \nat{\frac{7}{12}}\) (6 va 4 ning umumiy karralisi 12).

Javob noto'g'ri kasr: \(\frac{3}{4} + \frac{5}{6} = \frac{9}{12} + \frac{10}{12} = \frac{19}{12} = \nat{1\frac{7}{12}}\).

Butundan ayirish: 1 kg undan \(\frac{1}{3}\) kg pishiriqqa, \(\frac{1}{4}\) kg qo'shniga berildi. \(1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\), \(\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{12} - \frac{3}{12} = \nat{\frac{5}{12}}\) kg qoldi.

2Qoidalar

Butunlar — butunlar bilan, kasrlar — kasrlar bilan. Faqat kasrlar avval bir xil maxrajga kelsin!
1-qoida · aralash sonlarni qo'shish
  1. Kasr qismlarni umumiy maxrajga keltiramiz.
  2. Butun qismlarni alohida, kasr qismlarni alohida qo'shamiz.
Namuna · \(1\frac{1}{4} + 2\frac{1}{3}\)

\(1\frac{1}{4} + 2\frac{1}{3} = 1\frac{3}{12} + 2\frac{4}{12} = (1 + 2) + \left(\frac{3}{12} + \frac{4}{12}\right) = \nat{3\frac{7}{12}}\)

2-qoida · kasr qism noto'g'ri bo'lib qolsa

Qo'shishdan keyin kasr qism noto'g'ri kasr bo'lsa, undan butun ajratib, butun qismga qo'shamiz.

Namuna · \(2\frac{5}{6} + 1\frac{3}{4}\)
1\(2\frac{5}{6} + 1\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{10}{12} + 1\frac{9}{12} = 3\frac{19}{12}\)
2\(\frac{19}{12} = \frac{12}{12} + \frac{7}{12} = 1\frac{7}{12}\) — bitta butun ajraldi.
3\(3\frac{19}{12} = 3 + 1\frac{7}{12} = \nat{4\frac{7}{12}}\)
3-qoida · aralash songa butun son qo'shish

Butun son faqat butun qismga qo'shiladi, kasr qism o'zgarmaydi: \(2\frac{3}{7} + 3 = \nat{5\frac{3}{7}}\).

4-qoida · aralash sonlarni ayirish

Kasr qismlarni umumiy maxrajga keltirib, butundan butunni, kasrdan kasrni ayiramiz.

Namuna · \(4\frac{5}{6} - 2\frac{1}{4}\)

\(4\frac{5}{6} - 2\frac{1}{4} = 4\frac{10}{12} - 2\frac{3}{12} = \nat{2\frac{7}{12}}\)

5-qoida · kasr qism yetmasa

Kamayuvchining kasr qismi ayriluvchinikidan kichik bo'lsa, kamayuvchining butun qismidan bitta butunni olib, uni kasrga aylantiramiz va kasr qismga qo'shamiz.

Namuna · \(5\frac{1}{4} - 2\frac{2}{3}\)
1Umumiy maxraj: \(5\frac{3}{12} - 2\frac{8}{12}\). \(\frac{3}{12}\) dan \(\frac{8}{12}\) ni ayirib bo'lmaydi.
2Bitta butunni olamiz: \(5\frac{3}{12} = 4 + \frac{12}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = 4\frac{15}{12}\).
3\(4\frac{15}{12} - 2\frac{8}{12} = \nat{2\frac{7}{12}}\)
6-qoida · butun sondan aralash sonni ayirish

Butun sondan bitta butunni olib, uni ayriluvchining maxraji bilan kasrga aylantiramiz.

Namuna · \(6 - 2\frac{4}{9}\)

\(6 - 2\frac{4}{9} = 5\frac{9}{9} - 2\frac{4}{9} = \nat{3\frac{5}{9}}\)

7-qoida · javobni chamalab tekshirish

Har bir aralash sonni eng yaqin butun songa yaxlitlab, og'zaki hisoblaymiz. Aniq javob chamaga yaqin bo'lishi kerak.

Namuna

\(3\frac{4}{5} + 2\frac{1}{10} \approx 4 + 2 = 6\). Aniq javob: \(3\frac{8}{10} + 2\frac{1}{10} = 5\frac{9}{10}\) — 6 ga yaqin, maqbul ✓

8-qoida · 2-usul: noto'g'ri kasr orqali

Aralash sonlarni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantirib, oddiy kasrlardek qo'shish (ayirish) ham mumkin. Javob yana aralash son qilib yoziladi.

Aralash sonni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantirish: butun × maxraj + surat — yangi surat, maxraj o'zgarmaydi: \(1\frac{2}{3} = \frac{1 \cdot 3 + 2}{3} = \frac{5}{3}\).

Namuna · \(1\frac{2}{3} + 2\frac{1}{2}\) ikki usulda

1-usul

\(1\frac{4}{6} + 2\frac{3}{6} = 3\frac{7}{6} = \nat{4\frac{1}{6}}\)

2-usul

\(\frac{5}{3} + \frac{5}{2} = \frac{10}{6} + \frac{15}{6} = \frac{25}{6} = \nat{4\frac{1}{6}}\)

3Rasmda ko'ramiz

Oltidan bir bo'laklarni sanang: oltita bo'lak — bitta butun doira!

Qo'shish: \(1\frac{4}{6} + 2\frac{3}{6} = 3\frac{7}{6} = 4\frac{1}{6}\)

+
=

4 ta oltidan bir va 3 ta oltidan bir — 7 ta oltidan bir. Ulardan 6 tasi bitta butun bo'ladi, bittasi qoladi.

Ayirish: \(3\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4}\) — bitta butunni 4 ta chorakka maydalaymiz:

=

\(3\frac{1}{4} = 2\frac{5}{4}\), so'ng \(2\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{2}{4} = 1\frac{1}{2}\)

4Namunalar

Namuna 1 · masofa

Sportchi A dan B gacha \(1\frac{3}{4}\) km, B dan C gacha \(2\frac{2}{5}\) km yugurdi. a) Jami qancha yugurdi? b) BC masofa AB dan qancha uzun?

a\(1\frac{3}{4} + 2\frac{2}{5} = 1\frac{15}{20} + 2\frac{8}{20} = 3\frac{23}{20} = \nat{4\frac{3}{20}}\) km. Chamalash: \(2 + 2 = 4\) ✓
b\(2\frac{2}{5} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{8}{20} - 1\frac{15}{20} = 1\frac{28}{20} - 1\frac{15}{20} = \nat{\frac{13}{20}}\) km
Namuna 2 · mato (ikki amal)

Tikuvchida \(5\frac{1}{2}\) m mato bor edi. U \(2\frac{3}{4}\) m ishlatdi va yana \(1\frac{2}{3}\) m sotib oldi. Unda qancha mato bo'ldi?

1\(5\frac{1}{2} - 2\frac{3}{4} = 5\frac{2}{4} - 2\frac{3}{4} = 4\frac{6}{4} - 2\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{3}{4}\) m qoldi.
2\(2\frac{3}{4} + 1\frac{2}{3} = 2\frac{9}{12} + 1\frac{8}{12} = 3\frac{17}{12} = \nat{4\frac{5}{12}}\) m bo'ldi.
Namuna 3 · ayirmani qo'shing

\(2\frac{1}{6}\) ga \(3\frac{3}{4}\) va \(1\frac{1}{3}\) ning ayirmasini qo'shing.

1Ayirma: \(3\frac{3}{4} - 1\frac{1}{3} = 3\frac{9}{12} - 1\frac{4}{12} = 2\frac{5}{12}\).
2\(2\frac{1}{6} + 2\frac{5}{12} = 2\frac{2}{12} + 2\frac{5}{12} = \nat{4\frac{7}{12}}\)
Namuna 4 · chizmali model

Tarvuzning massasi 5 kg, u qovundan \(1\frac{5}{8}\) kg og'ir. Qovun necha kilogramm?

\(5 - 1\frac{5}{8} = 4\frac{8}{8} - 1\frac{5}{8} = \nat{3\frac{3}{8}}\) kg. Chamalash: \(5 - 2 = 3\) ✓

Namuna 5 · uch bosqich

Ko'k sim 9 m, u qizil simdan \(2\frac{1}{4}\) m uzun. Qizil sim qora simdan \(3\frac{2}{5}\) m uzun. Qora sim necha metr?

1Qizil: \(9 - 2\frac{1}{4} = 8\frac{4}{4} - 2\frac{1}{4} = 6\frac{3}{4}\) m.
2Qora: \(6\frac{3}{4} - 3\frac{2}{5} = 6\frac{15}{20} - 3\frac{8}{20} = \nat{3\frac{7}{20}}\) m.

5Diqqat! Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

Maxrajlar hech qachon qo'shilmaydi!
Xato ⟶ To'g'ri
  1. Maxrajlarni ham qo'shib yuborish.
    \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{5}\)  →  \(\frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5}{6}\)
  2. Yangi maxrajga keltirganda suratni ko'paytirmaslik.
    \(\frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{12}\)  →  \(\frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{12}\)
  3. Kasr qism noto'g'ri kasr bo'lib qolgan javob.
    \(3\frac{19}{12}\)  →  \(4\frac{7}{12}\)
  4. Kasr qism yetmaganda, kichigidan kattasini ayirish.
    \(5\frac{3}{12} - 2\frac{8}{12} = 3\frac{5}{12}\)  →  \(4\frac{15}{12} - 2\frac{8}{12} = 2\frac{7}{12}\)
  5. Butun sondan ayirganda kasr qismni unutish.
    \(6 - 2\frac{4}{9} = 4\frac{4}{9}\)  →  \(5\frac{9}{9} - 2\frac{4}{9} = 3\frac{5}{9}\)

Avval chamalayman, keyin hisoblayman — javob chamaga yaqinmi?

7Matnli masalalar

Masalalarni yeching (javobni chamalab tekshiring)
  1. Idishda \(3\frac{2}{5}\) l suv bor. To'lishi uchun yana \(1\frac{3}{4}\) l suv kerak. Idishning sig'imi necha litr?
    Javobni ko'rish\(3\frac{2}{5} + 1\frac{3}{4} = 3\frac{8}{20} + 1\frac{15}{20} = 4\frac{23}{20} = 5\frac{3}{20}\) l
  2. Oshxonada \(7\frac{1}{4}\) kg un bor edi. Pishiriq uchun \(3\frac{2}{3}\) kg ishlatildi. Qancha un qoldi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(7\frac{3}{12} - 3\frac{8}{12} = 6\frac{15}{12} - 3\frac{8}{12} = 3\frac{7}{12}\) kg
  3. Aziz \(3\frac{1}{2}\) km yugurdi, Bobur undan \(\frac{5}{6}\) km kam. Bobur qancha yugurdi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(3\frac{3}{6} - \frac{5}{6} = 2\frac{9}{6} - \frac{5}{6} = 2\frac{4}{6} = 2\frac{2}{3}\) km
  4. Arqon \(5\frac{2}{3}\) m va \(3\frac{3}{4}\) m li ikki bo'lakka kesildi. Arqon necha metr edi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(5\frac{8}{12} + 3\frac{9}{12} = 8\frac{17}{12} = 9\frac{5}{12}\) m
  5. Opaning bo'yi \(1\frac{1}{2}\) m, ukasi undan \(\frac{3}{10}\) m past. Ukasining bo'yi qancha?
    Javobni ko'rish\(1\frac{5}{10} - \frac{3}{10} = 1\frac{2}{10} = 1\frac{1}{5}\) m
  6. 6 m matodan \(3\frac{5}{8}\) m parda tikildi. Necha metr mato qoldi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(5\frac{8}{8} - 3\frac{5}{8} = 2\frac{3}{8}\) m
  7. Zahro \(3\frac{7}{10}\) km yugurdi, Guli undan \(1\frac{1}{2}\) km ko'p. Ikkalasi birgalikda qancha yugurdi?
    Javobni ko'rishGuli: \(3\frac{7}{10} + 1\frac{5}{10} = 4\frac{12}{10} = 5\frac{1}{5}\) km. Jami: \(3\frac{7}{10} + 5\frac{2}{10} = 8\frac{9}{10}\) km
  8. Idishda \(4\frac{1}{4}\) l suv bor edi. \(2\frac{3}{5}\) l ishlatildi, so'ng \(1\frac{7}{10}\) l quyildi. Idishda necha litr suv bo'ldi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(4\frac{5}{20} - 2\frac{12}{20} = 3\frac{25}{20} - 2\frac{12}{20} = 1\frac{13}{20}\); \(1\frac{13}{20} + 1\frac{14}{20} = 2\frac{27}{20} = 3\frac{7}{20}\) l
  9. Do'kondan \(3\frac{3}{10}\) m oq, \(2\frac{1}{2}\) m qora va \(1\frac{3}{4}\) m qizil mato olindi. Jami necha metr?
    Javobni ko'rish\(3\frac{6}{20} + 2\frac{10}{20} + 1\frac{15}{20} = 6\frac{31}{20} = 7\frac{11}{20}\) m
  10. 1 kg kartoshkaning \(\frac{3}{8}\) kg i sho'rvaga, \(\frac{1}{4}\) kg i qovurmaga ishlatildi. Qancha kartoshka qoldi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(1 - \frac{3}{8} = \frac{5}{8}\); \(\frac{5}{8} - \frac{2}{8} = \frac{3}{8}\) kg

8O'ylab ko'ring

Kasr qismlarga diqqat qiling — butun hosil bo'lyaptimi?
Xatoni toping

Lola yechdi: «\(3\frac{1}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 2\frac{2}{4}\), chunki \(3 - 1 = 2\), \(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4}\)». Xatosi nimada?

Javobni ko'rishU kichik kasrdan kattasini emas, kattasidan kichigini ayirgan — kasrlar o'rnini almashtirib yuborgan. To'g'risi: \(2\frac{5}{4} - 1\frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{2}{4} = 1\frac{1}{2}\). Chamalash ham yordam beradi: \(3 - 2 = 1\), javob 2 dan kichik bo'lishi kerak.
Qonuniyat

\(1\frac{1}{2}\), \(2\frac{3}{4}\), \(4\), \(5\frac{1}{4}\), ... — keyingi ikkita sonni yozing.

Javobni ko'rishHar safar \(1\frac{1}{4}\) qo'shilyapti: \(5\frac{1}{4} + 1\frac{1}{4} = 6\frac{1}{2}\), \(6\frac{1}{2} + 1\frac{1}{4} = 7\frac{3}{4}\).
Butun yig'indi

\(1\frac{1}{3} + 2\frac{1}{4} + \square = 5\). Qaysi son tushib qolgan?

Javobni ko'rish\(1\frac{1}{3} + 2\frac{1}{4} = 3\frac{7}{12}\). \(5 - 3\frac{7}{12} = 4\frac{12}{12} - 3\frac{7}{12} = 1\frac{5}{12}\).
