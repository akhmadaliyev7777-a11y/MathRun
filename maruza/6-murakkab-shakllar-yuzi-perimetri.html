Murakkab shakllarning yuzi va perimetri

Hovuz atrofidagi yo'lka, gulzor, deraza, plitka naqshi — ko'p shakllar kvadrat, uchburchak, yarim va chorak doiralardan tuzilgan. Ularning yuzi va perimetrini bo'laklarga ajratib topamiz.

1-qism

Mavzu

Usul · murakkab shaklning yuzi
1Shaklni tanish bo'laklarga ajrating: kvadrat, to'g'ri to'rtburchak, uchburchak, doira, yarim doira, chorak doira.
2Har bir bo'lakning yuzini toping.
3Bo'yalgan soha bo'laklardan yig'ilgan bo'lsa — yuzlarni qo'shing. Bo'yalgan soha katta shakldan bo'lak olib tashlanib hosil bo'lgan bo'lsa — katta shakl yuzidan olib tashlangan bo'lak yuzini ayiring.

Kerakli formulalar: kvadrat \(a \cdot a\); to'g'ri to'rtburchak \(a \cdot b\); uchburchak \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \text{asos} \cdot \text{balandlik}\); doira \(\pi r^2\); yarim doira \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \pi r^2\); chorak doira \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \pi r^2\).

1-misol · kvadratdan chorak doira

Kvadratning tomoni 10 cm. Uning ichiga radiusi 10 cm bo'lgan chorak doira chizildi. Bo'yalgan soha yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Kvadrat: \(10 \cdot 10 = 100\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Chorak doira: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 78,5\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Bo'yalgan soha: \(100 - 78,5 = \nat{21,5}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · doiradan uchburchak

Chorak doiraning radiusi 20 cm. Bo'yalgan soha yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Chorak doira: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 20 \cdot 20 = 314\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Uchburchakning to'g'ri burchak tomonlari — radiuslar: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 20 \cdot 20 = 200\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Bo'yalgan soha: \(314 - 200 = \nat{114}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Bo'yalgan soha — kvadratning chorak doiradan ortib qolgan qismi. Shuning uchun yuzlar ayiriladi.
Usul · bo'laklarni birlashtirish
1Radiusi bir xil bo'lgan 4 ta chorak doira birga — bitta butun doira.
2Radiusi bir xil bo'lgan 2 ta yarim doira birga — bitta butun doira.
3Shuning uchun bo'laklarning yuzini alohida hisoblash shart emas: bitta doiraning yuzini topish yetarli.

2 ta yarim doira

1-misol · burchaklarda 4 ta chorak doira

Kvadratning tomoni 20 cm. Uning har bir burchagida bir xil chorak doira chizilgan, ular bir-biriga tegib turadi. Bo'yalgan soha yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Chorak doiralar tomonning o'rtasida tegadi: radius \(20 : 2 = 10\) cm.
24 ta chorak doira — bitta doira: \(3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 314\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Kvadrat \(20 \cdot 20 = 400\ \text{cm}^2\). Bo'yalgan soha: \(400 - 314 = \nat{86}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · hovuz atrofidagi yo'lka

Hovuz to'g'ri to'rtburchak va ikki yarim doiradan iborat: to'g'ri qismining uzunligi 20 m, eni 10 m. Atrofida eni 1 m bo'lgan yo'lka bor. Yo'lkaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Ikkita to'g'ri bo'lak: \(2 \cdot 20 \cdot 1 = 40\ \text{m}^2\).
2Ikki chetdagi yarim halqalar birga — bitta halqa. Radiuslar: ichki \(10 : 2 = 5\) m, tashqi \(5 + 1 = 6\) m.
3Halqa: \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 - 3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 113,04 - 78,5 = 34,54\ \text{m}^2\).
4Yo'lka: \(40 + 34,54 = \nat{74,54}\ \text{m}^2\).
Yuz — shakl egallagan joy, kvadrat birlikda: \(\text{cm}^2\), \(\text{m}^2\). Perimetr — shaklni o'rab turgan chiziq uzunligi: cm, m.
Usul · murakkab shaklning perimetri
1Shaklni o'rab turgan chiziqni boshidan oxirigacha aylanib chiqing. Qaysi bo'laklari yoy, qaysilari to'g'ri chiziq ekanini belgilang.
2Har bir yoy qaysi aylananing qismi ekanini aniqlang (yarmimi, choragimi) va uzunligini toping.
3Barcha yoy va to'g'ri chiziqlar uzunliklarini qo'shing. Shakl ichidagi chiziqlar perimetrga kirmaydi.
1-misol · javob \(\pi\) orqali

Chorak doiradan yarim doira kesib olindi. Chorak doiraning radiusi 10 cm. Bo'yalgan soha perimetrini \(\pi\) orqali ifodalang.

1Katta yoy — aylananing choragi: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 2 \cdot 10 \cdot \pi = 5\pi\) cm.
2Kichik yoy — diametri 10 cm bo'lgan aylananing yarmi: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot \pi = 5\pi\) cm.
3Chap tomonda radius qoldi: 10 cm. Perimetr: \(5\pi + 5\pi + 10 = \nat{(10\pi + 10)}\) cm.
2-misol · uchburchak va 3 ta yarim doira

Gulzor to'g'ri burchakli uchburchak (tomonlari 5 m, 12 m, 13 m) va uning tomonlariga chizilgan 3 ta yarim doiradan iborat. Gulzorning a) perimetrini, b) yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

aChegarada faqat 3 ta yoy bor: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot (5 + 12 + 13) \cdot \pi = 15\pi \approx \nat{47,1}\) m.
bYarim doiralar: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 84,5 = 132,665\ \text{m}^2\) (bu yerda \(2,5 \cdot 2,5 + 6 \cdot 6 + 6,5 \cdot 6,5 = 84,5\)). Uchburchak: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 12 = 30\ \text{m}^2\). Jami \(162,665 \approx \nat{162,67}\ \text{m}^2\).
2-qism

Misollar ishlash

1-misol · shaklning yarmi bilan ishlash

Kvadratning tomoni 10 cm. Uning ikki qarama-qarshi uchidan radiusi 10 cm bo'lgan chorak aylanalar chizildi. Bo'yalgan yaproq shaklining yuzi va perimetrini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Punktir diagonal yaproqni ikkita teng yarmiga ajratadi. Har bir yarmi — chorak doiradan uchburchak olib tashlangan qism (1-qismdagi kabi).
2Yarmi: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 78,5 - 50 = 28,5\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Yaproq: \(2 \cdot 28,5 = \nat{57}\ \text{cm}^2\).
4Perimetr — ikkita chorak yoy: \(2 \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot 2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 = \nat{31,4}\) cm. Diagonal ichkarida — qo'shilmaydi.

Boshqa yo'l: ikkala chorak doiraning yuzi \(2 \cdot 78,5 = 157\ \text{cm}^2\). Ularda yaproq ikki marta sanalgan, kvadrat esa bir marta: \(157 - 100 = 57\ \text{cm}^2\). Javob bir xil.

2-misol · bo'lakni ko'chirish

Kvadratning tomoni 10 cm. Uning chap tomonidan yarim doira o'yib olinib, o'ng tomoniga yopishtirildi. Hosil bo'lgan shaklning yuzi va perimetrini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Qancha olingan bo'lsa, shuncha qo'shilgan. Yuz kvadratnikidek qoladi: \(10 \cdot 10 = \nat{100}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Chegarada: tepada va pastda kvadrat tomonlari \(10 + 10 = 20\) cm, chapda va o'ngda ikkita yarim aylana yoyi: \(2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 = 31,4\) cm.
3Perimetr: \(20 + 31,4 = \nat{51,4}\) cm.

Yuz o'zgarmadi, perimetr esa o'zgardi: kvadratning perimetri 40 cm edi.

3-misol · doiraning \(\frac{1}{3}\) qismi

Doira 3 ta teng bo'lakka bo'lindi. Doiraning radiusi 6 cm. Bitta bo'lakning perimetri va yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Bo'lakning yoyi — aylananing \(\frac{1}{3}\) qismi: \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot 2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 = 12,56\) cm.
2Perimetr: yoy va ikkita radius: \(12,56 + 6 + 6 = \nat{24,56}\) cm.
3Yuz — doira yuzining \(\frac{1}{3}\) qismi: \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = \nat{37,68}\ \text{cm}^2\).

Yarim doira — \(\frac{1}{2}\), chorak doira — \(\frac{1}{4}\). Bo'lak doiraning qaysi qismi bo'lsa, yoyi ham, yuzi ham shu qismga ko'paytiriladi.

4-misol · bir-biriga tegib turgan uchta doira

Radiusi 7 cm bo'lgan uchta bir xil doira bir-biriga tegib turibdi. Ular orasidagi bo'yalgan soha perimetrini toping (\(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\)).

1Markazlarni tutashtiramiz: \(AB = BC = AC = 7 + 7 = 14\) cm. Uchburchak teng tomonli, har bir burchagi \(60°\).
2\(60°\) — to'liq aylananing \(360° : 60° = 6\) dan bir qismi. Har bir yoy aylananing \(\frac{1}{6}\) qismi, uchta yoy — \(\frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\) qismi.
3Perimetr: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 7 = \nat{22}\) cm.
Yuzda ichki chiziq bo'laklarni ajratishga yordam beradi. Perimetrda esa u umuman sanalmaydi!
Diqqat!
  1. Perimetrga shakl ichidagi chiziq qo'shilmaydi. To'g'ri to'rtburchak (8 dm × 5 dm) va uning 8 dm li tomoni ustida yarim doira: \(8 + 5 + 5 + 8 + 12,56\)   \(8 + 5 + 5 + 12,56 = 30,56\) dm
  2. Bo'yalgan soha topilganda kattadan kichik ayiriladi, aksincha emas: \(78,5 - 100\)   \(100 - 78,5 = 21,5\ \text{cm}^2\)
  3. Kvadrat burchagidagi chorak doiraning radiusi kvadrat tomoniga teng. 4 ta chorak doira tomon o'rtasida tegib tursa, radius — tomonning yarmi.
  4. Ichkariga botgan yoy ham perimetrga kiradi. Yuzda esa uning bo'lagi ayiriladi.
  5. Yuz \(\text{cm}^2\), \(\text{m}^2\) da, perimetr cm, m da yoziladi.

Javoblar

Mustahkamlash

1-masalaa) \(6 \cdot 6 - \frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 36 - 28,26 = 7,74\ \text{cm}^2\); b) \(10 \cdot 4 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 40 + 6,28 = 46,28\ \text{cm}^2\); c) doiraning radiusi 4 cm: \(8 \cdot 8 - 3,14 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 64 - 50,24 = 13,76\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-masalaChorak doira: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 21 \cdot 21 = 346,5\ \text{cm}^2\). Yaproq: \(2 \cdot 346,5 - 21 \cdot 21 = 693 - 441 = 252\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr: \(2 \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 21 = 66\) cm.
3-masalaRadius 8 dm. Yarim doira: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 100,48\ \text{dm}^2\). Uchburchakning balandligi — radius: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 16 \cdot 8 = 64\ \text{dm}^2\). Bo'yalgan soha: \(100,48 - 64 = 36,48\ \text{dm}^2\).
4-masalaIkkita yarim doira — bitta doira, radiusi 6 cm: \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 113,04\ \text{cm}^2\). Yuz: \(30 \cdot 12 - 113,04 = 360 - 113,04 = 246,96\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr: ikkita uzun tomon \(30 + 30 = 60\) cm va ikkita yarim aylana yoyi \(3,14 \cdot 12 = 37,68\) cm; jami \(97,68\) cm.
5-masala4 ta yarim doira — 2 ta doira, radiusi 8 cm: \(2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 401,92\ \text{cm}^2\). Yuz: \(16 \cdot 16 + 401,92 = 256 + 401,92 = 657,92\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr — 4 ta yarim yoy, ya'ni 2 ta aylana: \(2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 16 = 100,48\) cm.
6-masalaBo'lak — doiraning \(\frac{1}{6}\) qismi. Yoy: \(\frac{1}{6} \cdot 2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 15 = 15,7\) cm; perimetr: \(15,7 + 15 + 15 = 45,7\) cm. Yuz: \(\frac{1}{6} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 15 \cdot 15 = 117,75\ \text{cm}^2\).
7-masalaPerimetr: \(13 + 13 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot \pi = (26 + 5\pi)\) cm. Yuz: uchburchak \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 12 = 60\ \text{cm}^2\), yarim doira \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot \pi = 12,5\pi\ \text{cm}^2\); jami \((60 + 12,5\pi)\ \text{cm}^2\).
8-masalaChorak yoy: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 7 = 11\) m. Perimetr: pastda \(7 + 20 + 7 = 34\) m, tepada 20 m, ikkita yoy 22 m; jami \(34 + 20 + 22 = 76\) m. Yuz: \(20 \cdot 7 + 2 \cdot \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 7 \cdot 7 = 140 + 77 = 217\ \text{m}^2\).
9-masalaYuz: \(30 \cdot 20 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 7 \cdot 7 = 600 - 77 = 523\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr: \(30 + 20 + 20 + (30 - 14) + \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 14 = 30 + 40 + 16 + 22 = 108\) cm.
10-masalaTanga: \(3,14 \cdot 15 \cdot 15 = 706,5\ \text{mm}^2\). Teshik: \(3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 78,5\ \text{mm}^2\). Kvadrat 4 ta uchburchakdan iborat, har biri \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 12,5\ \text{mm}^2\): kvadrat \(50\ \text{mm}^2\). Bo'yalgan qism: \(706,5 - 78,5 + 50 = 678\ \text{mm}^2\).
11-masalaMarkazlar orasidagi masofa \(2 + 2 = 4\) cm — teng tomonli uchburchak, burchaklari \(60°\). Har bir yoy aylananing \(\frac{1}{6}\) qismi, uchtasi — \(\frac{1}{2}\) qismi: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 2 = 6,28\) cm.

Mustaqil ish

1-masalaIkkita kesilgan chorak doira birga — yarim doira, radiusi 6 cm; pastga ham radiusi 6 cm bo'lgan yarim doira qo'shildi. Yuz kvadratnikidek: \(12 \cdot 12 = 144\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr: yon tomonlarda \(6 + 6 = 12\) cm, ikkita chorak yoy \(2 \cdot 9,42 = 18,84\) cm, pastdagi yarim yoy \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 12 = 18,84\) cm; jami \(12 + 18,84 + 18,84 = 49,68\) cm.
2-masala\(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 20 \cdot 20 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 12 \cdot 12 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 628 - 226,08 - 100,48 = 301,44\ \text{cm}^2\).
3-masalaIkkita kichik yarim doira: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 4,5 \cdot 4,5 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 31,7925 + 56,52 = 88,3125\ \text{dm}^2\). Uchburchak: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 9 \cdot 12 = 54\ \text{dm}^2\). Katta yarim doira: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 7,5 \cdot 7,5 = 88,3125\ \text{dm}^2\). Bo'yalgan soha \(=\) ikkita kichik yarim doira \(+\) uchburchak \(-\) katta yarim doira \(= 88,3125 + 54 - 88,3125 = 54\ \text{dm}^2\) — uchburchak yuziga teng!
4-masalaHar bir gulbarg — ikkita chorak doiradan uchburchak olib tashlangan qism (yaproq kabi), radiusi 6 cm. Gulbargning yarmi: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 28,26 - 18 = 10,26\ \text{cm}^2\). 4 ta gulbarg: \(8 \cdot 10,26 = 82,08\ \text{cm}^2\). Perimetr: 8 ta chorak yoy, ya'ni 2 ta aylana: \(2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 = 75,36\) cm.
5-masalaa) Kichik gilam (radiusi 0,5 m): \(3,14 \cdot 0,5 \cdot 0,5 = 0,785\ \text{m}^2\). Katta gilam (radiusi 1,5 m): \(3,14 \cdot 1,5 \cdot 1,5 = 7,065\ \text{m}^2\). \(7,065 : 0,785 = 9\) marta katta. b) Katta gilam 8 marta qimmat, lekin yuzi 9 marta katta. 8 ta kichik gilam narxiga katta gilam olinadi, 8 ta kichik gilam esa atigi \(8 \cdot 0,785 = 6,28\ \text{m}^2\). Katta gilamning har \(1\ \text{m}^2\) i arzonroq.
6-masalaG'ildirak bir aylanganda mashina aylana uzunligicha yuradi: \(2 \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 14 = 88\) cm. Ikkala mashina birga bir aylanishda \(2 \cdot 88 = 176\) cm yaqinlashadi. \(7,04\) m \(= 704\) cm; \(704 : 176 = 4\) aylanish. Vaqt: \(4 \cdot 2,5 = 10\) sekund.
7-masalaPerimetr: katta yarim yoy \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 20 = 31,4\) cm va ikkita kichik yarim yoy \(2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 = 31,4\) cm; jami \(62,8\) cm. 9-masalaHar bir yarim halqada bir cheti tashqi (radiusi 11 m), ikkinchisi ichki (9 m). A dan boshlangan sim birinchi burilishda tashqi chetda, ikkinchisida ichki chetda bo'ladi. A dagi sim: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 11 \cdot \pi + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 \cdot 9 \cdot \pi = 11\pi + 9\pi = 20\pi\) m. B dagi sim: \(9\pi + 11\pi = 20\pi\) m. Ikkala sim teng.
