Yarim va chorak doira yuzi

Yarim doira shaklidagi gulzorga qancha maysa ekiladi? Chorak doira shaklidagi burchak javoni ustiga qancha laminat kerak? Buni bilish uchun yarim va chorak doiraning yuzini topamiz.

1-qism

Mavzu

Usul · yarim doira yuzi

\(\text{Radius} = \text{diametr} : 2\)

\(\text{Yarim doira yuzi} = \frac{1}{2} \times \pi r^2\)

1-misol · diametr berilgan

Yarim doiraning diametri 8,4 dm. Uning yuzini toping (\(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\)).

1Radius: \(8,4 : 2 = 4,2\) dm.
2\(\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 4,2 \cdot 4,2\). Maxrajdagi 7 qisqaradi: \(4,2 : 7 = 0,6\).
3\(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 22 \cdot 0,6 \cdot 4,2 = 11 \cdot 0,6 \cdot 4,2 = \nat{27,72}\ \text{dm}^2\).
2-misol · radius berilgan

Yarim doiraning radiusi 6 cm. Uning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1\(r^2 = 6 \cdot 6 = 36\).
2Yuzi: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 36 = 1,57 \cdot 36 = \nat{56,52}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Usul · chorak doira yuzi

\(\text{Chorak doiraning yuzi} = \frac{1}{4} \times \pi r^2\)

1-misol · radius berilgan

Chorak doiraning radiusi 20 cm. Uning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1\(r^2 = 20 \cdot 20 = 400\).
2Yuzi: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 400 = 3,14 \cdot 100 = \nat{314}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · diametr berilgan

Doiraning diametri 11,2 cm. Uning choragi yuzini toping (\(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\)).

1Radius: \(11,2 : 2 = 5,6\) cm.
2\(\frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{22}{7} \cdot 5,6 \cdot 5,6\). Maxrajdagi 7 qisqaradi: \(5,6 : 7 = 0,8\).
3\(0,8 : 4 = 0,2\). Yuzi: \(22 \cdot 0,2 \cdot 5,6 = \nat{24,64}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Yarim doirada doira yuzini 2 ga, chorak doirada 4 ga bo'lamiz. Radius esa o'zgarmaydi — uni ikkiga bo'lmang.
Usul · bo'laklardan tuzilgan shakl
1Shakl qanday bo'laklardan tuzilganini aniqlang: yarim doiralar, chorak doiralar.
2Har bir bo'lakning yuzini toping.
3Yuzlarni qo'shing. Bo'laklar birga butun doira hosil qilsa, darhol \(\pi r^2\) ni toping.
1-misol · ikkita yarim doira

Shakl ikkita bir xil yarim doiradan tuzilgan. Har birining diametri 12 cm. Shaklning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Ikkita bir xil yarim doira birga — bitta butun doira. Radius: \(12 : 2 = 6\) cm.
2Yuzi: \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = \nat{113,04}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · yarim va chorak doira

Shakl yarim doira va chorak doiradan tuzilgan, markazi O, radiusi 8 cm. Shaklning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Yarim va chorak birga: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\) — doiraning \(\frac{3}{4}\) qismi.
2Doira: \(3,14 \cdot 8 \cdot 8 = 200,96\ \text{cm}^2\). Uning \(\frac{3}{4}\) qismi: \(200,96 : 4 \cdot 3 = \nat{150,72}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Radiuslari teng bo'lsa, 2 ta yarim doira yoki 4 ta chorak doira birga bitta butun doira bo'ladi.
Usul · bo'yalgan sohaning yuzi
1Katta shaklning yuzini toping.
2Kesib olingan (bo'yalmagan) bo'laklarning yuzini toping.
3Katta shakl yuzidan kesib olingan bo'laklar yuzini ayiring.
1-misol · uchta yarim doira

Diametri 22 cm bo'lgan yarim doiradan diametrlari 10 cm va 12 cm bo'lgan ikkita yarim doira kesib olindi. Bo'yalgan sohaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Katta yarim doira, \(r = 11\) cm: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 11 \cdot 11 = 189,97\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Kichiklari, \(r = 5\) cm va \(r = 6\) cm: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 39,25\ \text{cm}^2\) va \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 56,52\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Bo'yalgan soha: \(189,97 - 39,25 - 56,52 = \nat{94,2}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · chorak doiradan yarim doira

Radiusi 6 cm bo'lgan chorak doiradan diametri 6 cm bo'lgan yarim doira kesib olindi. Qolgan qismning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Chorak doira: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 28,26\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Yarim doira, \(r = 6 : 2 = 3\) cm: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = 14,13\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Qolgan qism: \(28,26 - 14,13 = \nat{14,13}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-qism

Misollar ishlash

1-misol · kalkulyatordagi \(\pi\), yaxlitlash

Yarim doira shaklidagi derazaning diametri 1,6 m. Uning oynasi yuzini kalkulyatordagi \(\pi\) tugmasi bilan toping. Javobni yuzdan birlar xonasigacha yaxlitlang.

1Radius: \(1,6 : 2 = 0,8\) m.
2Kalkulyatorda: \(\pi \times 0,8 \times 0,8 \div 2\). Ekranda \(1,0053…\) chiqadi.
3Verguldan keyingi uchinchi raqam 5: \(1,0053… \approx \nat{1,01}\ \text{m}^2\).
2-misol · doiraning \(\frac{3}{4}\) qismi

Doiradan uning choragi kesib olindi. Doiraning radiusi 2 m. Qolgan shaklning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Qolgan shakl — doiraning \(\frac{3}{4}\) qismi. Doira: \(3,14 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 12,56\ \text{m}^2\), \(\frac{3}{4}\) qismi: \(12,56 : 4 \cdot 3 = \nat{9,42}\ \text{m}^2\).
2Ikkinchi yo'l — ayirish: doira \(3,14 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 12,56\ \text{m}^2\), chorak \(12,56 : 4 = 3,14\ \text{m}^2\). \(12,56 - 3,14 = 9,42\ \text{m}^2\).
3-misol · yarim doira va ikkita chorak doira

Lola gulining rasmi diametri 10 dm bo'lgan yarim doira va radiusi 5 dm bo'lgan ikkita chorak doiradan tuzilgan. Shaklning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Yarim doiraning radiusi: \(10 : 2 = 5\) dm. Chorak doiralarniki ham 5 dm — radiuslar teng.
2Ulushlar: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = 1\) — bo'laklar birga bitta butun doiraga teng.
3Yuzi: \(3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = \nat{78,5}\ \text{dm}^2\).

Radiuslar har xil bo'lsa, bo'laklar butun doira hosil qilmaydi. Unda har bir bo'lakning yuzi alohida topilib, qo'shiladi.

4-misol · bir bo'lak qo'shildi, xuddi shunday bo'lak olindi

Doiraning diametri 20 cm. Ichidagi egri chiziq ikkita bir xil yarim aylanadan iborat. Bo'yalgan sohaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Bo'yalgan soha = katta yarim doira + chapdagi kichik yarim doira − o'ngdagi kichik yarim doira.
2Kichik yarim doiralar bir xil: qo'shilgani olinganiga teng. Demak, bo'yalgan soha katta yarim doiraga teng.
3\(r = 20 : 2 = 10\) cm. \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = \nat{157}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Yuzga diametr qo'shilmaydi! Diametr perimetrga qo'shilardi.
Diqqat!
  1. Diametr berilsa, avval radiusni toping. Chorak doira, \(d = 20\) cm: \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 20 \cdot 20\)   \(\frac{1}{4} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 78,5\ \text{cm}^2\)
  2. \(r^2 = r \cdot r\). Yarim doira, \(r = 5\) cm: \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 2\)   \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 = 39,25\ \text{cm}^2\)
  3. Yuzga diametr yoki radius qo'shilmaydi. Yarim doira, \(r = 6\) cm: \(56,52 + 12\)   \(56,52\ \text{cm}^2\)
  4. Yarim doirada yuz ikkiga bo'linadi, radius emas. \(r = 10\) cm: \(3,14 \cdot 5 \cdot 5\)   \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3,14 \cdot 10 \cdot 10 = 157\ \text{cm}^2\)
  5. Yuz kvadrat birlikda yoziladi: 157 cm   \(157\ \text{cm}^2\)
