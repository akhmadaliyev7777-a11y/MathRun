O'rtacha tezlik

Avtobus shahar ichida sekin, katta yo'lda tez yuradi. Butun yo'lda uning tezligi qancha bo'lgan? Bunday savolga o'rtacha tezlik javob beradi.

1-qism

Mavzu

Eslab olaylik
  • Tezlik = masofa : vaqt;   masofa = tezlik · vaqt;   vaqt = masofa : tezlik.
  • 30 min = \(\frac{1}{2}\) soat,   15 min = \(\frac{1}{4}\) soat,   20 min = \(\frac{1}{3}\) soat,   45 min = \(\frac{3}{4}\) soat.
  • Aralash songa bo'lish: \(90 : 1\frac{1}{2} = 90 : \frac{3}{2} = 90 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 60\).
Qoida · o'rtacha tezlik

O'rtacha tezlik = jami masofa : jami vaqt

Avtobus goh tez, goh sekin yuradi. Agar u butun yo'lni o'rtacha tezlikda bir tekis yursa, manzilga xuddi shu vaqtda yetib borardi.
1-misol · uch qism

Velosipedchi bog' yo'lida to'xtamasdan uch qism yo'l yurdi: 1200 m ni 4 minutda, 1500 m ni 6 minutda, 900 m ni 2 minutda. Uning o'rtacha tezligi qancha?

1Jami masofa: \(1200 + 1500 + 900 = 3600\) m.
2Jami vaqt: \(4 + 6 + 2 = 12\) min.
3O'rtacha tezlik: \(3600 : 12 = \nat{300}\) m/min.
2-misol · bir qismda tezlik berilgan

Elektropoyezd shahar ichida 30 km ni 1 soatda yurdi. Shahardan chiqqach, 150 km ni 75 km/soat tezlikda yurdi. Uning butun yo'ldagi o'rtacha tezligi qancha?

1Ikkinchi qism vaqti: \(150 : 75 = 2\) soat.
2Jami masofa: \(30 + 150 = 180\) km. Jami vaqt: \(1 + 2 = 3\) soat.
3O'rtacha tezlik: \(180 : 3 = \nat{60}\) km/soat.
3-misol · javob butun emas

Sportchi stadionda ikki aylana yugurdi. Bir aylana 400 m. Birinchi aylanani 60 sekundda, ikkinchisini 65 sekundda yugurdi.
a) U jami qancha vaqt yugurdi?
b) Uning o'rtacha tezligini toping.

aJami vaqt: \(60 + 65 = 125\) s, ya'ni \(\nat{2}\) min \(\nat{5}\) s.
bJami masofa: \(400 + 400 = 800\) m.
O'rtacha tezlik: \(800 : 125 = 6\) (qoldiq 50), ya'ni \(6\frac{50}{125} = \nat{6\frac{2}{5}}\) m/s yoki \(\nat{6,4}\) m/s.
4-misol · o'rtacha tezlik ma'lum

Samolyot A shahardan C shaharga B shahar ustidan qo'nmasdan uchdi. A dan B gacha 1500 km ni 2 soatda uchdi, B dan C gacha yana 1650 km uchdi. Butun yo'ldagi o'rtacha tezligi 700 km/soat. B dan C gacha uchishga qancha vaqt ketgan?

1Jami masofa: \(1500 + 1650 = 3150\) km.
2Jami vaqt = jami masofa : o'rtacha tezlik: \(3150 : 700 = 4\frac{1}{2}\) soat.
3B dan C gacha: \(4\frac{1}{2} - 2 = \nat{2\frac{1}{2}}\) soat, ya'ni 2 soat 30 min.
5-misol · yo'lning qismi berilgan

Sayyohlar avtobusi yo'lning \(\frac{2}{5}\) qismini tog'da 3 soatda bosib o'tdi. Qolgan qismini tekislikda 60 km/soat tezlikda 2 soatda yurdi. Avtobusning butun yo'ldagi o'rtacha tezligi qancha?

1Qolgan qism — yo'lning \(1 - \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3}{5}\) qismi. Uning uzunligi: \(60 \cdot 2 = 120\) km.
2\(\frac{3}{5}\) qism 120 km bo'lsa, \(\frac{1}{5}\) qism \(120 : 3 = 40\) km. Butun yo'l: \(40 \cdot 5 = 200\) km.
3Jami vaqt: \(3 + 2 = 5\) soat. O'rtacha tezlik: \(200 : 5 = \nat{40}\) km/soat.
O'rtacha tezlik formulasidan yana ikkitasi chiqadi: jami masofa = o'rtacha tezlik · jami vaqt; jami vaqt = jami masofa : o'rtacha tezlik.
2-qism

Misollar ishlash

1-misol · borib kelish

Sayyohlar A lagerdan B ko'lgacha 12 km yo'lni tepalikka 3 km/soat tezlikda ko'tarilib bosib o'tdi. Xuddi shu yo'ldan 6 km/soat tezlikda pastga tushib qaytdi. Butun yo'ldagi o'rtacha tezlik qancha?

1Ko'tarilish vaqti: \(12 : 3 = 4\) soat. Tushish vaqti: \(12 : 6 = 2\) soat.
2Jami masofa: \(12 + 12 = 24\) km (yo'l ikki marta bosildi). Jami vaqt: \(4 + 2 = 6\) soat.
3O'rtacha tezlik: \(24 : 6 = \nat{4}\) km/soat.

Tezliklarning o'rtachasi \((3 + 6) : 2 = 4,5\) km/soat — bu javob noto'g'ri. Sayyohlar 4 soat sekin, faqat 2 soat tez yurdi. Sekin yurilgan vaqt ko'p bo'lgani uchun o'rtacha tezlik kichikroq chiqdi.

2-misol · vaqt soatlar bilan berilgan

Paroxod soat 13:30 da A pristandan jo'nadi. 15:00 da B pristanga yetdi va to'xtamasdan suzib, 16:00 da C pristanga yetdi. A dan B gacha 36 km, B dan C gacha 24 km. Paroxodning o'rtacha tezligi qancha?

1Jami vaqt: 13:30 dan 16:00 gacha — 2 soat 30 min = \(2\frac{1}{2}\) soat = 2,5 soat.
2Jami masofa: \(36 + 24 = 60\) km.
3O'rtacha tezlik: \(60 : 2,5 = 600 : 25 = \nat{24}\) km/soat.

Agar savol boshqacha bo'lsa: «o'rtacha tezligi 24 km/soat, 13:30 dan 16:00 gacha suzdi — qancha yo'l bosdi?» — unda jami masofa = o'rtacha tezlik · jami vaqt: \(24 \cdot 2,5 = 60\) km.

3-misol · jadval bilan

Velosipedchi va mopedchi P qishloqdan R qishloqqa Q qishloq orqali bordi. P dan Q gacha 20 km, Q dan R gacha 25 km. Jadvalni to'ldiring.

Diqqat!
  1. Tezliklarning o'rtachasini olmang. 12 km ni 3 km/soat, qaytishda 6 km/soat: \((3 + 6) : 2 = 4,5\) km/soat   \(24 : 6 = 4\) km/soat
  2. Tezligi berilgan qismning vaqtini avval toping, keyin hamma vaqtlarni qo'shing. 30 km — 1 soatda, 150 km — 75 km/soat tezlikda: faqat 1 soatni olish: \(180 : 1 = 180\)   \(180 : (1 + 2) = 60\) km/soat
  3. Minutlarni soatga to'g'ri aylantiring: 2 soat 30 min = 2,3 soat   2 soat 30 min = 2,5 soat
  4. O'rtacha tezlik eng kichik va eng katta tezlik orasida bo'ladi. 3 km/soat va 6 km/soat yurilgan bo'lsa, o'rtacha tezlik 3 dan katta, 6 dan kichik.

5-qism

Javoblar

Mustahkamlash

1-masalaa) \(90 + 60 = 150\) km, \(2 + 1 = 3\) soat, \(150 : 3 = 50\) km/soat. b) \(400 + 600 + 200 = 1200\) m, \(5 + 10 + 5 = 20\) min, \(1200 : 20 = 60\) m/min.
2-masalaJami masofa hammasida \(12 + 18 = 30\) km. Velosipedchi: 2 soat, \(30 : 2 = 15\) km/soat. Samokatchi: \(1\frac{1}{2}\) soat, \(30 : 1\frac{1}{2} = 20\) km/soat. Otliq: \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{3}{4} = 1\frac{1}{4}\) soat, \(30 : 1\frac{1}{4} = 30 \cdot \frac{4}{5} = 24\) km/soat.
3-masala\(4 + 8 = 12\) km, \(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{3}{4} = 1\) soat. \(12 : 1 = 12\) km/soat.
4-masalaBorib kelish: \(9 + 9 = 18\) km. Vaqt: \(18 : 12 = 1\frac{1}{2}\) soat, ya'ni 1 soat 30 min.
5-masala\(2 + 2 = 4\) km, \(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\) soat. \(4 : \frac{2}{3} = 4 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 6\) km/soat.
6-masalaa) Tekis yo'lda: \(65 \cdot 2 = 130\) km. Jami: \(130 + 95 = 225\) km. b) \(225 : 4\frac{1}{2} = 225 \cdot \frac{2}{9} = 50\) km/soat.
7-masalaa) \(108 : 36 = 3\) soat; jami \(3 + 3 = 6\) soat. b) \(180 : 6 = 30\) km/soat.
8-masalaa) Oxirgi 1 soatda: \(12 \cdot 1 = 12\) km — bu yo'lning \(\frac{1}{3}\) qismi. Butun yo'l: \(12 \cdot 3 = 36\) km. b) Jami vaqt 4 soat, \(36 : 4 = 9\) km/soat.
9-masalaa) Yo'lning yarmi: \(280 : 2 = 140\) km. O'rtadagi bekatdan keyin: \(140 : 56 = 2\frac{1}{2}\) soat. Jami: \(1\frac{1}{2} + 2\frac{1}{2} = 4\) soat. b) \(280 : 4 = 70\) km/soat.
10-masalaBirinchi qism: \(60 : 40 = 1\frac{1}{2}\) soat. Jami vaqt: \(1\frac{1}{2} + 1 = 2\frac{1}{2}\) soat. \(90 : 2\frac{1}{2} = 90 \cdot \frac{2}{5} = 36\) km/soat.
11-masalaa) 09:40 dan 12:10 gacha — 2 soat 30 min = \(2\frac{1}{2}\) soat. \(64 \cdot 2\frac{1}{2} = 160\) km. b) Ikkinchi qism: \(160 - 90 = 70\) km, 11:10 dan 12:10 gacha — 1 soat. Tezligi 70 km/soat.

Mustaqil ish

1-masalaB dan C gacha: \(120 : 60 = 2\) soat. Jami: \(160 + 120 = 280\) km, \(2 + 2 = 4\) soat. \(280 : 4 = 70\) km/soat.
2-masala\(40 + 50 + 60 = 150\) s. \(150 : 150 = 1\) m/s.
3-masalaBozorgacha: \(15 \cdot 1 = 15\) km. Qaytish: \(15 : 10 = 1\frac{1}{2}\) soat. Jami: \(30\) km, \(2\frac{1}{2}\) soat. \(30 : 2\frac{1}{2} = 12\) km/soat (12,5 emas).
4-masalaJami masofa: \(50 + 160 = 210\) km. Jami vaqt: \(210 : 70 = 3\) soat. Ikkinchi qism: \(3 - 1 = 2\) soat.
5-masalaQolgan \(\frac{3}{4}\) qism: \(30 \cdot 3 = 90\) km. \(\frac{1}{4}\) qism: \(90 : 3 = 30\) km, butun yo'l \(30 \cdot 4 = 120\) km. Vaqt: \(2 + 3 = 5\) soat. \(120 : 5 = 24\) km/soat.
6-masala10:05 dan 10:35 gacha — 30 min = \(\frac{1}{2}\) soat. Masofa: \(6 + 4 = 10\) km. \(10 : \frac{1}{2} = 20\) km/soat.
7-masalaNodira tezliklarning o'rtachasini olgan. Borish: \(90 : 60 = 1\frac{1}{2}\) soat, qaytish: \(90 : 30 = 3\) soat. Jami: 180 km, \(4\frac{1}{2}\) soat. \(180 : 4\frac{1}{2} = 40\) km/soat.
8-masala3 soatda jami: \(14 \cdot 3 = 42\) km. Dastlabki 2 soatda: \(15 \cdot 2 = 30\) km. Oxirgi soatda: \(42 - 30 = 12\) km, ya'ni 12 km/soat.
