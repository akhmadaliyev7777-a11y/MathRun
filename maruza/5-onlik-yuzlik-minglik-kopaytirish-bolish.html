Sonni o'nlik, yuzlik, mingliklarga ko'paytirish va bo'lish

20, 300, 4000 kabi sonlarga qanday tez ko'paytirish va bo'lish mumkin?

1-qism

Mavzu

Eslab olaylik
  • 10, 100, 1000 ga ko'paytirishda sonning oxiriga bitta, ikkita, uchta nol yoziladi: \(46 \cdot 100 = 4600\).
  • 10, 100, 1000 ga bo'lishda oxiridan bitta, ikkita, uchta nol o'chiriladi: \(5800 : 100 = 58\).
  • Ko'paytuvchilar o'rni almashtirilsa, ko'paytma o'zgarmaydi: \(8 \cdot 10 = 10 \cdot 8\).
  • Yaxlitlash: \(58 \approx 60\), \(412 \approx 400\). Belgi \(\approx\) — «taxminan teng».
10 ga ko'paytirish oson. 20 ga-chi?
Usul · ko'paytirish

\(20 = 2 \cdot 10\),   \(300 = 3 \cdot 100\),   \(4000 = 4 \cdot 1000\).

\(37 \cdot 20 = 37 \cdot 2 \cdot 10 = 74 \cdot 10 = \nat{740}\)

1-misol · yuzlikka

\(74 \cdot 300\) ni hisoblang.

1\(300 = 3 \cdot 100\).
2\(74 \cdot 3 = 222\).
3\(222 \cdot 100 = \nat{22 200}\).
2-misol · boshqa tartibda

\(156 \cdot 30\) ni hisoblang.

1\(30 = 10 \cdot 3\), shuning uchun avval 10 ga: \(156 \cdot 10 = 1560\).
2Keyin 3 ga: \(1560 \cdot 3 = \nat{4680}\).
Javob taxminan qancha chiqishini bilsangiz, xatoni kalkulyatorsiz ham sezasiz.
Usul · chamalab tekshirish

Chamalash — sonlarni yaxlitlab, javobni taxminan topish. Aniq javob chamaga yaqin bo'lsa, u maqbul javob (to'g'riga o'xshaydigan javob).

Chamalashda sonni birinchi raqamigacha yaxlitlaymiz: \(58 \approx 60\), \(412 \approx 400\), \(3120 \approx 3000\). Yaxlit son (\(300\), \(80\)) o'zgarmaydi.

\(58 \cdot 300\):   \(58 \approx 60\),   \(60 \cdot 300 = 18 000\),   demak \(58 \cdot 300 \approx 18 000\).

Aniq javob: \(58 \cdot 3 \cdot 100 = 174 \cdot 100 = \nat{17 400}\) — 18 000 ga yaqin, maqbul.

Bo'lishda ham ikkala sonni yaxlitlaymiz. \(3680 : 80\):   \(3680 \approx 4000\),   \(4000 : 80 = 400 : 8 = 50\). Aniq javob: \(368 : 8 = \nat{46}\) — 50 ga yaqin, maqbul.

1-misol · javob maqbul

\(65 \cdot 40 = 2600\). Bu javob maqbulmi?

1\(65 \approx 70\),   \(70 \cdot 40 = 2800\).
22600 soni 2800 ga yaqin — maqbul.
2-misol · xato topildi

Sardor \(213 \cdot 300 = 6390\) deb yozdi. Chamalab tekshiring.

1\(213 \approx 200\),   \(200 \cdot 300 = 60 000\).
26390 soni 60 000 dan juda kichik — javob xato.
3To'g'risi: \(213 \cdot 3 = 639\),   \(639 \cdot 100 = \nat{63 900}\).
Bo'lishda qaysi biri avval: 10 gami yoki 8 gami?
Usul · bo'lish

\(720 : 80 = 720 : 10 : 8 = 72 : 8 = \nat{9}\)

Avval 10 ga bo'lamiz. Son 8 ga qoldiqsiz bo'linsa, avval 8 ga bo'lsa ham bo'ladi (2-misol).

Tekshirish: \(9 \cdot 80 = 720\).

1-misol · minglikka

15000 da uchta nol. Ikkala sondan uchtadan nol o'chiramiz: \(45 : 5\).
2\(45 : 5 = \nat{9}\). Tekshirish: \(9 \cdot 5000 = 45 000\).
2-misol · nollar soni har xil

\(18 000 : 30\) ni hisoblang.

118 000 da uchta nol, 30 da bitta. Faqat bittadan o'chiramiz: \(1800 : 3\).
2\(1800 : 3 = \nat{600}\). Tekshirish: \(600 \cdot 30 = 18 000\).

Misollar ishlash

1-misol · yaxlit son oldinda

\(5000 \cdot 46\) ni hisoblang va chamalab tekshiring.

1O'rnini almashtiramiz: \(46 \cdot 5000\).
2\(46 \cdot 5 = 230\).
3\(230 \cdot 1000 = \nat{230 000}\).
4Chamalash: \(46 \approx 50\), \(50 \cdot 5000 = 250 000\) — yaqin, maqbul.
2-misol · ikkala son yaxlit

\(80 \cdot 500\) ni hisoblang.

1\(500 = 5 \cdot 100\), shuning uchun \(80 \cdot 500 = 80 \cdot 5 \cdot 100\).
2\(80 \cdot 5 = 400\) (bunda \(8 \cdot 5 = 40\) ning o'zida ham nol bor).
3\(400 \cdot 100 = \nat{40 000}\).
3-misol · bo'lishni chamalash

\(3120 : 60\) ni hisoblang. Javob maqbul ekanini ko'rsating.

1Chamalaymiz: \(3120 \approx 3000\), \(3000 : 60 = 300 : 6 = 50\).
2Bittadan nol o'chiramiz: \(3120 : 60 = 312 : 6\).
3\(312 : 6 = \nat{52}\) — 50 ga yaqin, maqbul.
4Tekshirish: \(52 \cdot 60 = 3120\).

\(50 \cdot 60\) da nechta nol bo'ladi: ikkitami yoki uchtami?
Diqqat!
  1. Nol yozilmay qolgan:
    \(26 \cdot 30 = 78\)  →  \(26 \cdot 30 = 780\)
  2. \(5 \cdot 6 = 30\) dagi nol tushib qolgan:
    \(50 \cdot 60 = 300\)  →  \(50 \cdot 60 = 3000\)
  3. Nollar teng o'chirilmagan:
    \(8000 : 40 = 8 : 4 = 2\)  →  \(8000 : 40 = 800 : 4 = 200\)
  4. 100 ga bo'lish unutilgan:
    \(2400 : 300 = 2400 : 3 = 800\)  →  \(24 : 3 = 8\)
  5. Chamalangan javob aniq javob emas:
    \(58 \cdot 300 = 18 000\)  →  \(58 \cdot 300 \approx 18 000\), aniq javob 17 400
