Sonning moduli. Modul qatnashgan tenglama va tengsizliklar

Termometr −6 °C ni ham, 6 °C ni ham ko'rsatishi mumkin. Ikkala son ham noldan 6 birlik uzoqda turibdi. Sonning noldan qanchalik uzoqligini uning moduli ko'rsatadi.

1-qism

Mavzu

Ta'rif · sonning moduli

1) Musbat sonning moduli shu sonning o'ziga teng.

2) Manfiy sonning moduli unga qarama-qarshi songa teng.

3) Nolning moduli nolga teng: \(|0| = 0\).

Shunday qilib, son modulining ta'rifi formula yordamida qisqacha shunday yoziladi:

\(|a| = \begin{cases} a, & \text{agar } a \ge 0 \text{ bo'lsa;} \\ -a, & \text{agar } a \lt 0 \text{ bo'lsa.} \end{cases}\)

1-misol · sonning moduli
1\(|14| = 14\) — musbat son, moduli o'ziga teng.
2\(|{-}9| = 9\) — manfiy son, uning qarama-qarshisi 9. Xuddi shunday \(\left|{-}\frac{4}{7}\right| = \frac{4}{7}\), \(\ |{-}0,8| = 0,8\).
2-misol · ifodaning qiymati
1\(|{-}6| + |2,5| - |{-}1,5| = 6 + 2,5 - 1,5 = \nat{7}\).
2\(x = 3\) bo'lsa: \(|x - 8| = |3 - 8| = |{-}5| = \nat{5}\).
Ta'rif · modulning geometrik ma'nosi

\(|a|\) — \(a\) sonning moduli, geometrik nuqtayi nazardan, 0 nuqtadan \(a\) sonni tasvirlovchi nuqtagacha bo'lgan masofadir.

1-misol · masofani topish

\(A\) nuqta \(-4,5\) sonni, \(B\) nuqta 3 sonni tasvirlaydi. Qaysi biri 0 ga yaqinroq?

1\(|{-}4,5| = 4,5\), \(|3| = 3\). \(3 \lt 4,5\), demak \(\nat{B}\) nuqta 0 ga yaqinroq.
2-misol · nuqtani topish

Son o'qida 0 dan 6 birlik uzoqlikda qaysi nuqtalar yotadi?

10 dan o'ngga 6 birlik — 6, chapga 6 birlik — \(-6\). Javob: \(\nat{6}\) va \(\nat{-6}\).
Usul · modul belgisi ostida noma'lum qatnashgan tenglama
1Modul ichidagi ifoda \(\ge 0\) bo'lsin: modul belgisini tushirib qoldiramiz va hosil bo'lgan tenglamani yechamiz.
2Modul ichidagi ifoda \(\lt 0\) bo'lsin: ifodani o'ng tomondagi sonning qarama-qarshisiga tenglab yechamiz.
3Ikkala holda topilgan sonlar — tenglamaning ildizlari.
1-misol · modul ichida faqat noma'lum

\(|x| = 9\) tenglamani yeching.

1\(x \ge 0\) bo'lsin: \(|x| = x\), tenglama \(x = 9\).
2\(x \lt 0\) bo'lsin: \(|x| = -x\), tenglama \(-x = 9\), bundan \(x = -9\).
3Javob: \(x_1 = \nat{9}\), \(x_2 = \nat{-9}\). Ikkalasi ham 0 dan 9 birlik uzoqda.
2-misol · modul ichida ifoda

\(|2x - 5| = 7\) tenglamani yeching.

1\(2x - 5 \ge 0\) bo'lsin: \(2x - 5 = 7\), \(2x = 12\), \(x = 6\).
2\(2x - 5 \lt 0\) bo'lsin: \(2x - 5 = -7\), \(2x = -2\), \(x = -1\).
3Tekshiramiz: \(|12 - 5| = 7\), \(|{-}2 - 5| = 7\). Javob: \(x_1 = \nat{6}\), \(x_2 = \nat{-1}\).
Qoida · «≤» belgili modulli tengsizlik

Ushbu \(|x| \le a\), bunda \(a \gt 0\), tengsizlikni 0 nuqtadan \(a\) dan katta bo'lmagan masofada yotuvchi barcha \(x\) nuqtalar, ya'ni \([-a;\ a]\) kesmaning nuqtalari qanoatlantiradi.

\([-a;\ a]\) kesma — ushbu \(-a \le x \le a\) tengsizlikni qanoatlantiruvchi \(x\) sonlar to'plami.

Demak, \(|x| \le a\) tengsizlik \(-a \le x \le a\) qo'sh tengsizlikning ayni o'zini bildiradi, bunda \(a \gt 0\).

1-misol · modul ichida faqat noma'lum

\(|x| \lt 4\) tengsizlikni yeching.

10 dan 4 birlikdan kam masofadagi nuqtalar: \(\nat{-4 \lt x \lt 4}\).
2\(-4\) va 4 kirmaydi (\(|4| = 4\), 4 esa 4 dan kichik emas), shuning uchun ular bo'sh doiracha.
2-misol · modul ichida ifoda

\(|2x - 1| \le 5\) tengsizlikni yeching.

1Qo'sh tengsizlik: \(-5 \le 2x - 1 \le 5\). U ushbu sistemaning xuddi o'zi: \(\begin{cases} 2x - 1 \le 5, \\ 2x - 1 \ge -5. \end{cases}\)
2Birinchisidan \(x \le 3\), ikkinchisidan \(x \ge -2\). Javob: \(\nat{-2 \le x \le 3}\).
Qoida · «≥» belgili modulli tengsizlik

Ushbu \(|x| \ge a\), bunda \(a \gt 0\), tengsizlikni 0 nuqtadan \(a\) dan kichik bo'lmagan masofada yotuvchi barcha \(x\) nuqtalar to'plami, ya'ni \(x \ge a\) va \(x \le -a\) nurlarning nuqtalari qanoatlantiradi.

1-misol · modul ichida faqat noma'lum

\(|x| \gt 2,5\) tengsizlikni yeching.

10 dan 2,5 birlikdan uzoqroq nuqtalar ikki tomonda yotadi: \(\nat{x \lt -2,5}\) yoki \(\nat{x \gt 2,5}\).
2-misol · modul ichida ifoda

\(|x + 3| \ge 4\) tengsizlikni yeching.

1\(x + 3 \ge 0\) bo'lsin: \(x + 3 \ge 4\), \(x \ge 1\).
2\(x + 3 \lt 0\) bo'lsin: \(-(x + 3) \ge 4\), ya'ni \(x + 3 \le -4\), \(x \le -7\).
3Javob: \(\nat{x \le -7}\) yoki \(\nat{x \ge 1}\).
«\(\le\)» bo'lsa, yechim 0 ga yaqin nuqtalar — bitta kesma. «\(\ge\)» bo'lsa, 0 dan uzoq nuqtalar — ikkita nur.
Qoida · o'ng tomonda nol yoki manfiy son

Agar \(|x| \le a\) tengsizlikda \(a\) son nolga teng bo'lsa, u holda tengsizlik \(x = 0\) dan iborat birgina (yagona) yechimga ega bo'ladi; bordi-yu, agar \(a \lt 0\) bo'lsa, u holda tengsizlik yechimlarga ega bo'lmaydi.

Agar \(|x| \ge a\) tengsizlikda \(a\) son noldan kichik yoki unga teng bo'lsa, u holda istalgan son uning yechimi bo'ladi.

1-misol · «≤» belgi
1\(|x - 2| \le 0\) faqat \(x - 2 = 0\) da bajariladi: \(\nat{x = 2}\) — yagona yechim.
2\(|3x + 1| \le -4\) — yechim yo'q: modul manfiy bo'lmaydi.
2-misol · «≥» belgi
1\(|5 - x| \ge -2\) — istalgan son: modul doim \(\ge 0\), demak \(-2\) dan katta.
2\(|x + 7| \ge 0\) — ham istalgan son.
2-qism

Misollar ishlash

Tengsizlikni manfiy songa bo'lsangiz, belgini teskarisiga almashtiring: «\(\lt\)» o'rniga «\(\gt\)», «\(\le\)» o'rniga «\(\ge\)».
1-misol · noma'lum oldida minus

\(|6 - x| = 4\) tenglamani yeching.

1\(6 - x \ge 0\) bo'lsin: \(6 - x = 4\), bundan \(x = 2\).
2\(6 - x \lt 0\) bo'lsin: \(6 - x = -4\), bundan \(x = 10\).
3Son o'qida tekshiramiz: \(|6 - x|\) — \(x\) va 6 nuqtalar orasidagi masofa. 6 dan 4 birlik uzoqlikda 2 va 10 yotadi.
4Javob: \(x_1 = \nat{2}\), \(x_2 = \nat{10}\).
Diqqat

\(|x - a|\) — son o'qida \(x\) va \(a\) nuqtalar orasidagi masofa. Masalan, \(|7 - 3| = 4\) va \(|3 - 7| = 4\): 3 va 7 nuqtalar orasida 4 birlik bor.

Hayotda: lift \(-2\)-qavatdan 3-qavatga chiqsa ham, qaytib tushsa ham 5 qavat yuradi (rasmda).

2-misol · kasr koeffitsientli tenglama

\(\left|\frac{1}{2}x + \frac{1}{3}\right| = \frac{5}{6}\) tenglamani yeching.

1Modul ichidagi ifoda \(\ge 0\): \(\frac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{3} = \frac{5}{6}\). Ayiramiz: \(\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{1}{2}\), ya'ni \(\frac{1}{2}x = \frac{1}{2}\), \(x = 1\).
2Modul ichidagi ifoda \(\lt 0\): \(\frac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{3} = -\frac{5}{6}\). Bundan \(\frac{1}{2}x = -\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{3}\), ya'ni \(\frac{1}{2}x = -\frac{7}{6}\).
3Ikkala tomonni 2 ga ko'paytiramiz: \(x = -\frac{7}{3} = -2\frac{1}{3}\).
4Javob: \(x_1 = \nat{1}\), \(x_2 = \nat{-2\frac{1}{3}}\).
3-misol · butun yechimlar

\(|7 - 2x| \lt 3\) tengsizlikni yeching va uning butun yechimlarini toping.

1Qo'sh tengsizlik yozamiz: \(-3 \lt 7 - 2x \lt 3\).
2Uchala qismdan 7 ni ayiramiz: \(-10 \lt -2x \lt -4\).
3\(-2\) ga bo'lamiz, belgilar teskarisiga o'zgaradi: \(5 \gt x \gt 2\), ya'ni \(\nat{2 \lt x \lt 5}\).
4Butun yechimlar: \(\nat{3}\) va \(\nat{4}\). 2 va 5 kirmaydi — uchlari bo'sh doiracha.
4-misol · yozuvni almashtirish
1\(|x| \le 0,6\) ni qo'sh tengsizlik bilan yozamiz: \(-0,6 \le x \le 0,6\).
2\(-3,4 \lt x \lt 3,4\) ni modul bilan yozamiz: chetlari qarama-qarshi sonlar, demak \(|x| \lt 3,4\).
3\(|x| \gt 5\) ni qo'sh tengsizlik bilan yozib bo'lmaydi: yechim ikkita nur, \(x \lt -5\) yoki \(x \gt 5\).

Hayotda: sog'lom odamning harorati \(t\) 36,6 °C dan ko'pi bilan 0,5 °C ga farq qiladi (rasmda).

5-misol · «≥» belgi, ikki holat

\(|2x + 1| \ge 5\) tengsizlikni yeching.

1\(2x + 1 \ge 0\) bo'lsin. Sistemani yechamiz:
\(\begin{cases} 2x + 1 \ge 0, \\ 2x + 1 \ge 5 \end{cases} \Rightarrow x \ge 2.\)
2\(2x + 1 \lt 0\) bo'lsin. Unda \(-(2x + 1) \ge 5\), ya'ni \(2x + 1 \le -5\). Sistemani yechamiz:
\(\begin{cases} 2x + 1 \lt 0, \\ 2x + 1 \le -5 \end{cases} \Rightarrow x \le -3.\)
3Javob: \(\nat{x \le -3}\) yoki \(\nat{x \ge 2}\).

Ikki holatni qisqa yozish ham mumkin: \(2x + 1 \ge 5\) yoki \(2x + 1 \le -5\).

Diqqat!
  1. \(|x| = 5\): \(x = 5\)   \(x = 5\) yoki \(x = -5\)
  2. \(|x - 3| = -2\): \(x = 1\) yoki \(x = 5\)   ildiz yo'q — modul manfiy bo'lmaydi.
  3. \(|x + 1| = 0\): \(x = 1\) yoki \(x = -1\)   \(x = -1\) — ildiz bitta, chunki \(x + 1 = 0\).
  4. \(|{-}x| = 3\): \(x = -3\)   \(x = 3\) yoki \(x = -3\) — \(|{-}x| = |x|\).
  5. \(|x| \lt 4\): \(x \lt 4\)   \(-4 \lt x \lt 4\) — \(-4\) va 4 kirmaydi.
  6. \(|x| \gt 4\): \(-4 \gt x \gt 4\)   \(x \lt -4\) yoki \(x \gt 4\)
  7. \(-8 \lt -2x \lt -2\) dan: \(4 \lt x \lt 1\)   \(1 \lt x \lt 4\) — \(-2\) ga bo'lganda belgilar teskarisiga o'zgaradi.
