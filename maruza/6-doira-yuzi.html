Doiraning yuzi

Dumaloq gilam polning qancha qismini egallaydi? Dumaloq stol ustini bo'yashga qancha bo'yoq ketadi? Buni bilish uchun doiraning yuzini topamiz.

1-qism

Mavzu

Usul · kataklar bilan chamalash

Radiusi 4 cm bo'lgan doira katakli qog'ozga chizildi. Har bir katak — \(1\ \text{cm}^2\).

1Doiraning choragini olamiz. Unda to'liq kataklar 8 ta (ko'k).
2Yarmidan ko'pi chorak ichida bo'lgan kataklar 5 ta (sariq). Ularni ham butun katak deb sanaymiz. Yarmidan kami ichida bo'lganlarini tashlab ketamiz.
3Chorak doira: \(8 + 5 = 13\), taqriban \(13\ \text{cm}^2\).
4Butun doira 4 ta shunday chorakdan iborat: \(4 \cdot 13 = \nat{52}\ \text{cm}^2\) (taqriban).
Usul · kvadratlar bilan solishtirish

Doira katta kvadrat ichida

Kichik kvadrat doira ichida

1Katta kvadrat tomoni \(r\) bo'lgan 4 ta kvadratchadan iborat: yuzi \(4 \cdot r \cdot r = 4r^2\). Doira undan kichik.
2Kichik kvadrat 4 ta uchburchakdan iborat. Har biri \(r \cdot r\) kvadratchaning yarmi. Kichik kvadratning yuzi \(2r^2\). Doira undan katta.
3Demak, doiraning yuzi \(2r^2\) dan katta, \(4r^2\) dan kichik — taqriban \(3r^2\).
Radiusi 4 cm bo'lsa, \(3 \cdot 4 \cdot 4 = 48\). Kataklar bilan esa 52 chiqqan edi. Ikkalasi ham taxminiy-ku!
Qoida · doiraning yuzi

Doirani teng bo'laklarga kesamiz. Bo'laklarni bir qatorga teramiz: birining uchi pastga, keyingisining uchi tepaga qaraydi.

Bo'laklar qancha ko'p bo'lsa, shakl to'g'ri to'rtburchakka shuncha o'xshaydi. Uning eni taqriban aylana uzunligining yarmiga, bo'yi — radiusga teng.

\(\text{Doiraning yuzi} \approx \text{to'g'ri to'rtburchakning yuzi}\)
\(= \text{eni} \times \text{bo'yi}\)
\(= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{aylana uzunligi} \times \text{radius}\)
\(= \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times \pi \times \text{radius} \times \text{radius}\)
\(= \pi \times \text{radius} \times \text{radius}\)

\(\text{Doira yuzi} = \pi r^2\)

1-misol · radius berilgan

Doiraning radiusi 3,5 cm.
a) Doiraning yuzini \(\pi\) orqali ifodalang.
b) \(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\) deb olib, yuzini hisoblang.

a\(\pi \cdot 3,5 \cdot 3,5 = \nat{12,25\pi}\ \text{cm}^2\).
b\(\frac{22}{7} \cdot 3,5 \cdot 3,5\). Avval \(3,5 : 7 = 0,5\). So'ng \(22 \cdot 0,5 \cdot 3,5 = \nat{38,5}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · diametr berilgan

Dumaloq oynaning diametri 40 cm. Oynaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Radius: \(40 : 2 = 20\) cm.
2Yuzi: \(3,14 \cdot 20 \cdot 20 = 3,14 \cdot 400 = \nat{1256}\ \text{cm}^2\).
Formulada radius turadi. Diametr berilsa, avval uni 2 ga bo'ling. Yuz kvadrat birliklarda yoziladi: \(\text{cm}^2\) yoki \(\text{m}^2\).
Usul · halqaning yuzi

Katta doiradan markazi bir xil bo'lgan kichik doira olib tashlansa, halqa qoladi.

1Katta doiraning yuzini toping.
2Kichik doiraning yuzini toping.
3Halqaning yuzi = katta doiraning yuzi − kichik doiraning yuzi.
1-misol · ikkala radius berilgan

Katta doiraning radiusi 12 cm, kichik doiraniki 7 cm. Bo'yalgan halqaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Katta doira: \(3,14 \cdot 12 \cdot 12 = 452,16\ \text{cm}^2\).
2Kichik doira: \(3,14 \cdot 7 \cdot 7 = 153,86\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Halqa: \(452,16 - 153,86 = \nat{298,3}\ \text{cm}^2\).
2-misol · halqaning eni berilgan

Dumaloq hovuzning radiusi 6 m. Uning atrofida eni 1 m bo'lgan yo'lka bor. Yo'lkaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Katta doiraning radiusi: \(6 + 1 = 7\) m. Yuzi: \(3,14 \cdot 7 \cdot 7 = 153,86\ \text{m}^2\).
2Hovuz: \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 113,04\ \text{m}^2\).
3Yo'lka: \(153,86 - 113,04 = \nat{40,82}\ \text{m}^2\).
2-qism

Misollar ishlash

1-misol · javob \(\pi\) orqali, diametr berilgan

Doiraning diametri 2,6 dm. Uning yuzini \(\pi\) orqali ifodalang.

1Radius: \(2,6 : 2 = 1,3\) dm.
2\(\pi \cdot 1,3 \cdot 1,3 = \nat{1,69\pi}\ \text{dm}^2\). \(\pi\) o'rniga son qo'yilmaydi — javob shu ko'rinishda qoladi.
2-misol · \(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\), avval qisqartiramiz

Doiraning radiusi 35 cm. Uning yuzini toping (\(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\)).

1\(\frac{22}{7} \cdot 35 \cdot 35\). Bitta 35 ni 7 ga bo'lamiz: \(35 : 7 = 5\).
2Qolgani: \(22 \cdot 5 \cdot 35 = 110 \cdot 35 = \nat{3850}\ \text{cm}^2\).

Radius 7 ga bo'linsa, \(\frac{22}{7}\) qulay: avval qisqartirsak, hisob osonlashadi.

3-misol · javobni yaxlitlash

Suv purkagich maysani radiusi 2,3 m bo'lgan doira shaklida sug'oradi. Sug'oriladigan joyning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)). Javobni yuzdan birlar xonasigacha yaxlitlang.

1\(2,3 \cdot 2,3 = 5,29\).
2\(3,14 \cdot 5,29 = 16,6106\ \text{m}^2\).
3Verguldan keyingi uchinchi raqam 0, u 5 dan kichik: \(16,6106 \approx \nat{16,61}\ \text{m}^2\).
4-misol · kvadratdan doira kesildi

Tomoni 50 cm bo'lgan kvadrat shaklidagi taxtadan eng katta doira kesib olindi. Ortib qolgan taxtaning yuzini toping (\(\pi = 3,14\)).

1Doira kvadratga tegib turadi: diametri kvadrat tomoniga teng, 50 cm. Radius: \(50 : 2 = 25\) cm.
2Doira: \(3,14 \cdot 25 \cdot 25 = 1962,5\ \text{cm}^2\). Kvadrat: \(50 \cdot 50 = 2500\ \text{cm}^2\).
3Ortib qolgan qism: \(2500 - 1962,5 = \nat{537,5}\ \text{cm}^2\).
\(r^2\) ni \(2r\) deb o'qib yuborish oson. \(r^2 = r \cdot r\)!
Diqqat!
  1. \(r^2 = r \cdot r\). Masalan, \(r = 3\) cm bo'lsa: \(3,14 \cdot 3 \cdot 2\)   \(3,14 \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = 28,26\ \text{cm}^2\)
  2. Diametr berilsa, avval radiusni toping. \(d = 12\) cm: \(3,14 \cdot 12 \cdot 12\)   \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 113,04\ \text{cm}^2\)
  3. Yuz kvadrat birlikda yoziladi: 113,04 cm   \(113,04\ \text{cm}^2\)
  4. Halqada radiuslar ayirilmaydi, yuzlar ayiriladi. \(7\) m va \(6\) m: \(3,14 \cdot 1 \cdot 1\)   \(153,86 - 113,04 = 40,82\ \text{m}^2\)
  5. \(2\pi r\) — aylana uzunligi, \(\pi r^2\) — doiraning yuzi. Ularni adashtirmang.

Mustahkamlash

1-masalaa) \(r \cdot r\) (radius · radius); b) eni — aylana uzunligining yarmiga, bo'yi — radiusga; c) katta, kichik.
2-masalaTo'liq kataklar — 22 ta, yarmidan ko'pi bo'yalganlari — 6 ta: chorak \(\approx 28\ \text{cm}^2\). Doira \(\approx 4 \cdot 28 = 112\ \text{cm}^2\). (Formula bilan: \(3,14 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 113,04\ \text{cm}^2\).) Ikkita katak deyarli yarmigacha bo'yalgan: ularni ham sanasangiz, \(4 \cdot 30 = 120\ \text{cm}^2\) chiqadi. Chamalashda bunday farq bo'lishi mumkin.
3-masalaa) \(9\pi\ \text{cm}^2\); b) \(169\pi\ \text{m}^2\); c) \(r = 0,6\) m, \(0,36\pi\ \text{m}^2\); d) \(r = 20\) mm, \(400\pi\ \text{mm}^2\).
4-masalaa) \(\frac{22}{7} \cdot 21 \cdot 21 = 22 \cdot 3 \cdot 21 = 1386\ \text{cm}^2\); b) \(22 \cdot 0,1 \cdot 0,7 = 1,54\ \text{m}^2\); c) \(r = 1,4\) m, \(22 \cdot 0,2 \cdot 1,4 = 6,16\ \text{m}^2\); d) \(22 \cdot 8 \cdot 56 = 9856\ \text{mm}^2\).
5-masalaa) \(3,14 \cdot 9 = 28,26\ \text{m}^2\); b) \(3,14 \cdot 0,25 = 0,785\ \text{m}^2\); c) \(r = 30\) cm, \(3,14 \cdot 900 = 2826\ \text{cm}^2\); d) \(3,14 \cdot 1,44 = 4,5216\ \text{dm}^2\).
6-masalaa) \(3,14 \cdot 2,89 = 9,0746 \approx 9,07\ \text{cm}^2\); b) \(r = 4,5\) m, \(3,14 \cdot 20,25 = 63,585 \approx 63,6\ \text{m}^2\); c) \(3,14 \cdot 10,89 = 34,1946 \approx 34,19\ \text{dm}^2\).
