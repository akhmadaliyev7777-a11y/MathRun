Aralash sonni natural songa ko'paytirish

To'garak mashg'uloti \(1\frac{1}{4}\) soat davom etadi. Haftasiga 5 marta mashg'ulot bo'lsa, jami necha soat? Tomoni \(2\frac{3}{8}\) m bo'lgan kvadrat gilamning chetiga necha metr jiyak tikiladi?

1-qism

Mavzu

Eslab olaylik
  • Aralash son → noto'g'ri kasr: \(3\frac{2}{7} = \frac{3 \cdot 7 + 2}{7} = \frac{23}{7}\).
  • Noto'g'ri kasr → aralash son: \(\frac{59}{6} = 9\frac{5}{6}\) (\(59 : 6 = 9\), qoldiq 5).
  • Kasrni natural songa ko'paytirganda surat shu songa ko'paytiriladi, maxraj o'zgarmaydi: \(\frac{4}{9} \cdot 2 = \frac{8}{9}\).
  • Ko'paytirishdan oldin qisqartirish mumkin: \(\frac{5}{6} \cdot 4 = \frac{5 \cdot 4}{6}\) — 6 va 4 ni 2 ga qisqartiramiz: \(\frac{5 \cdot 2}{3} = \frac{10}{3}\).
\(1\frac{1}{4}\) soat — bir soat va yana chorak soat, ya'ni 15 minut.
Usul · noto'g'ri kasr orqali

\(1\frac{2}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{5}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{20}{3} = 6\frac{2}{3}\)

  • aralash sonni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantiring: \(1\frac{2}{3} = \frac{5}{3}\);
  • suratni songa ko'paytiring, maxrajni o'zgartirmang: \(\frac{5 \cdot 4}{3} = \frac{20}{3}\);
  • javobni aralash son qilib yozing: \(\frac{20}{3} = 6\frac{2}{3}\).

Diqqat!
  1. Faqat butun qism ko'paytirilgan:
    \(3\frac{2}{5} \cdot 2 = 6\frac{2}{5}\)  →  \(6 + \frac{4}{5} = 6\frac{4}{5}\)
  2. Butun qism ko'paytirilmagan:
    \(3\frac{1}{4} \cdot 4 = 3 + 1 = 4\)  →  \(12 + 1 = 13\)
  3. Butun qism maxrajga ko'paytirilmagan:
    \(4\frac{2}{7} = \frac{6}{7}\)  →  \(4\frac{2}{7} = \frac{4 \cdot 7 + 2}{7} = \frac{30}{7}\)
  4. Maxraj ham ko'paytirilgan:
    \(\frac{5}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{20}{12}\)  →  \(\frac{5}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{20}{3} = 6\frac{2}{3}\)
  5. Kasr qismdan butun ajratilmagan:
    \(2\frac{3}{5} \cdot 4 = 8\frac{12}{5}\)  →  \(8 + 2\frac{2}{5} = 10\frac{2}{5}\)
