Aralash sonni natural songa ko'paytirish

Aralash sonni natural songa ikki usulda ko'paytirishni — noto'g'ri kasrga aylantirib va butun hamda kasr qismlarni alohida ko'paytirib — hamda javobni chamalab tekshirishni o'rganamiz.

1Eslab olaylik — aralash son va noto'g'ri kasr

\(1\frac{1}{2}\) — bu bitta butun va yarimta. Uni kasr qilib yozsa bo'ladimi?
Eslab olaylik
  • Aralash sonni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantirish: butun × maxraj + surat — yangi surat, maxraj o'zgarmaydi: \(2\frac{5}{8} = \frac{2 \cdot 8 + 5}{8} = \frac{21}{8}\).
  • Noto'g'ri kasrni aralash songa aylantirish: suratni maxrajga bo'lamiz, bo'linma — butun qism, qoldiq — surat: \(\frac{147}{8} = 18\frac{3}{8}\) (\(147 : 8 = 18\), qoldiq 3).
  • Kasrni natural songa ko'paytirganda surat songa ko'paytiriladi, maxraj o'zgarmaydi: \(\frac{3}{2} \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{2}\).

2Qoidalar

Aralash sonni ko'paytirishning ikki yo'li bor — qulayini tanlang!
1-qoida · 1-usul: noto'g'ri kasr orqali
  1. Aralash sonni noto'g'ri kasrga aylantiramiz.
  2. Noto'g'ri kasrni natural songa ko'paytiramiz (qisqarsa — avval qisqartiramiz).
  3. Javobni aralash son yoki butun son qilib yozamiz.
Namuna · \(1\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3\) va \(3\frac{3}{4} \cdot 4\)

\(1\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 = \frac{3}{2} \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{2} = \nat{4\frac{1}{2}}\)

\(3\frac{3}{4} \cdot 4 = \frac{15}{\underset{1}{\cancel{4}}} \cdot \overset{1}{\cancel{4}} = \nat{15}\) — 4 va 4 ni qisqartirdik.

2-qoida · 2-usul: qismlarga ajratib
  1. Aralash sonni butun va kasr qismlarga ajratamiz.
  2. Har birini natural songa ko'paytiramiz.
  3. Topilgan ko'paytmalarni qo'shamiz.
Namuna · \(2\frac{1}{2} \cdot 4\)
1\(2\frac{1}{2} = 2 + \frac{1}{2}\)
2\(2 \cdot 4 = 8\), \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 = 2\)
3\(8 + 2 = \nat{10}\)

Kasr qismning ko'paytmasi noto'g'ri kasr chiqsa, undan butun ajratamiz: \(4\frac{2}{5} \cdot 10 = 40 + \frac{20}{5} = 40 + 4 = \nat{44}\); \(2\frac{3}{4} \cdot 2 = 4 + \frac{6}{4} = 4 + 1\frac{1}{2} = \nat{5\frac{1}{2}}\).

3-qoida · javobni chamalash

Natural sonni aralash sonning butun qismiga va bittaga katta songa ko'paytiramiz — javob shu ikki son orasida bo'lishi kerak.

Namuna · \(7 \cdot 2\frac{5}{8}\)
1Chamalaymiz: \(7 \cdot 2 = 14\), \(7 \cdot 3 = 21\) — javob 14 va 21 orasida.
2\(7 \cdot 2\frac{5}{8} = 7 \cdot \frac{21}{8} = \frac{147}{8} = \nat{18\frac{3}{8}}\) — 14 va 21 orasida ✓

Ko'paytuvchilarning o'rnini almashtirsa ham bo'ladi: \(7 \cdot 2\frac{5}{8} = 2\frac{5}{8} \cdot 7\).

3Rasmda ko'ramiz

Yarimtalarni juftlab qo'ysam — yangi butunlar hosil bo'ladi!

\(1\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3\): 3 nafar bolaning har biri \(1\frac{1}{2}\) tadan pirog sotib oldi.

+
+
=

3 ta butun va 3 ta yarimta. Ikkita yarimta — yana bitta butun: jami \(4\frac{1}{2}\) ta pirog.

\(2\frac{1}{2} \cdot 4\) qismlarga ajratib: butunlar alohida, yarimtalar alohida.

+

\(2 \cdot 4 = 8\) butun va \(\frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 = 2\) butun: \(8 + 2 = 10\).

4Namunalar

Namuna 1 · kvadratning perimetri

Kvadratning tomoni \(3\frac{3}{4}\) m. Uning perimetrini toping.

1-usul

\(3\frac{3}{4} \cdot 4 = \frac{15}{4} \cdot 4 = \nat{15}\) m

2-usul

\(3 \cdot 4 + \frac{3}{4} \cdot 4 = 12 + 3 = \nat{15}\) m

Namuna 2 · o'simliklarni sug'orish

O'simliklarni sug'orish uchun har kuni \(2\frac{5}{8}\) l suv ishlatiladi. 7 kunda qancha suv ishlatiladi?

1Chamalash: \(2 \cdot 7 = 14\), \(3 \cdot 7 = 21\).
2\(2\frac{5}{8} \cdot 7 = \frac{21 \cdot 7}{8} = \frac{147}{8} = \nat{18\frac{3}{8}}\) l ✓
Namuna 3 · xarid

1 kg olma 12 000 so'm turadi. \(2\frac{3}{4}\) kg olma qancha turadi?

\(12\,000 \cdot 2\frac{3}{4} = 12\,000 \cdot 2 + 12\,000 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 24\,000 + 9\,000 = \nat{33\,000}\) so'm.

Namuna 4 · kasr qismdan butun chiqadi

\(5\frac{1}{3} \cdot 6\) ni ikki usulda hisoblang.

1-usul

\(\frac{16}{\underset{1}{\cancel{3}}} \cdot \overset{2}{\cancel{6}} = \nat{32}\)

2-usul

\(5 \cdot 6 + \frac{1}{3} \cdot 6 = 30 + 2 = \nat{32}\)

Namuna 5 · yer maydonining yuzi

To'g'ri to'rtburchak shaklidagi yer maydonining o'lchamlari \(6\frac{3}{4}\) m va 6 m. Uning yuzini toping.

\(S = 6\frac{3}{4} \cdot 6 = \frac{27}{\underset{2}{\cancel{4}}} \cdot \overset{3}{\cancel{6}} = \frac{81}{2} = \nat{40\frac{1}{2}}\) m². Chamalash: \(6 \cdot 6 = 36\), \(7 \cdot 6 = 42\) ✓

5Diqqat! Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

Kasr qismni ham songa ko'paytirishni unutmang!
Xato ⟶ To'g'ri
  1. Faqat butun qismni ko'paytirish.
    \(2\frac{1}{3} \cdot 4 = 8\frac{1}{3}\)  →  \(8 + \frac{4}{3} = 9\frac{1}{3}\)
  2. Noto'g'ri kasrga aylantirishda butunni maxrajga ko'paytirmaslik.
    \(2\frac{1}{3} = \frac{3}{3}\)  →  \(2\frac{1}{3} = \frac{2 \cdot 3 + 1}{3} = \frac{7}{3}\)
  3. Maxrajni ham songa ko'paytirish.
    \(1\frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{6}\)  →  \(\frac{3}{2} \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{2} = 4\frac{1}{2}\)
  4. Kasr qismni noto'g'ri kasr ko'rinishida qoldirish.
    \(2\frac{3}{4} \cdot 2 = 4\frac{6}{4}\)  →  \(4 + 1\frac{1}{2} = 5\frac{1}{2}\)

7Matnli masalalar

Masalalarni yeching (javobni chamalab tekshiring)
  1. Bir qutida \(2\frac{3}{4}\) kg uzum bor. 4 ta qutida qancha uzum bor?
    Javobni ko'rish\(2\frac{3}{4} \cdot 4 = \frac{11}{4} \cdot 4 = 11\) kg
  2. Kvadratning tomoni \(4\frac{2}{3}\) m. Uning perimetrini toping.
    Javobni ko'rish\(4\frac{2}{3} \cdot 4 = \frac{56}{3} = 18\frac{2}{3}\) m
  3. 1 kg go'sht 90 000 so'm turadi. \(2\frac{1}{2}\) kg go'sht qancha turadi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(90\,000 \cdot 2 + 90\,000 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 180\,000 + 45\,000 = 225\,000\) so'm
  4. Aziz har kuni \(1\frac{3}{4}\) soat sport bilan shug'ullanadi. 6 kunda necha soat shug'ullanadi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(\frac{7}{4} \cdot 6 = \frac{21}{2} = 10\frac{1}{2}\) soat
  5. Teng tomonli uchburchakning tomoni \(3\frac{2}{5}\) dm. Uning perimetrini toping.
    Javobni ko'rish\(\frac{17}{5} \cdot 3 = \frac{51}{5} = 10\frac{1}{5}\) dm
  6. Bitta shishada \(1\frac{1}{4}\) l sharbat bor. 8 ta shishada necha litr sharbat bor?
    Javobni ko'rish\(1 \cdot 8 + \frac{1}{4} \cdot 8 = 8 + 2 = 10\) l
  7. Velosipedchi 1 soatda \(12\frac{1}{2}\) km yuradi. U 3 soatda qancha yo'l yuradi?
    Javobni ko'rish\(36 + \frac{3}{2} = 36 + 1\frac{1}{2} = 37\frac{1}{2}\) km
  8. Bitta g'ishtning massasi \(2\frac{2}{9}\) kg. 9 ta g'ishtning massasi qancha?
    Javobni ko'rish\(\frac{20}{9} \cdot 9 = 20\) kg
  9. To'g'ri to'rtburchakning bo'yi \(5\frac{1}{6}\) sm, eni 3 sm. Uning yuzi va perimetrini toping.
    Javobni ko'rishYuzi: \(\frac{31}{6} \cdot 3 = \frac{31}{2} = 15\frac{1}{2}\) sm². Perimetri: \((5\frac{1}{6} + 3) \cdot 2 = 8\frac{1}{6} \cdot 2 = 16\frac{1}{3}\) sm.
  10. 3 ta bankaning har birida \(2\frac{1}{3}\) l asal bor. 1 litr asal 80 000 so'm. Hamma asal qancha turadi?
    Javobni ko'rishAsal: \(2\frac{1}{3} \cdot 3 = 7\) l. Narxi: \(80\,000 \cdot 7 = 560\,000\) so'm.

8O'ylab ko'ring

Qaysi usul qulayroq — har safar o'ylab tanlang!
Xatoni toping

Nodira yechdi: «\(3 \cdot 4\frac{2}{9} = 12\frac{2}{9}\)». Xatosi nimada?

Javobni ko'rishU faqat butun qismni 3 ga ko'paytirgan, kasr qismni unutgan. To'g'risi: \(12 + \frac{2}{9} \cdot 3 = 12 + \frac{6}{9} = 12\frac{2}{3}\).
Qaysi usul qulay?

\(5\frac{1}{3} \cdot 6\) va \(5\frac{2}{7} \cdot 3\) ni hisoblang. Qaysi birida 2-usul, qaysi birida 1-usul qulayroq?

Javobni ko'rish\(5\frac{1}{3} \cdot 6 = 30 + 2 = 32\) — 6 soni 3 ga bo'linadi, 2-usul og'zaki bajariladi. \(5\frac{2}{7} \cdot 3 = \frac{37}{7} \cdot 3 = \frac{111}{7} = 15\frac{6}{7}\) — bu yerda ikkala usul ham bir xil: \(15 + \frac{6}{7}\).
Butun son chiqadimi?

\(3\frac{3}{8}\) ni qaysi eng kichik natural songa ko'paytirsak, javob butun son chiqadi?

Javobni ko'rish8 ga: \(\frac{27}{8} \cdot 8 = 27\). Kasr qism \(\frac{3}{8}\) butun bo'lishi uchun son 8 ga bo'linishi kerak.
